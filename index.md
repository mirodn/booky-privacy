# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

Booky is a simple app for keeping track of the books you read. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

- Booky has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
- Your library is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.
- When you search for a book, your search is sent to Google Books to find results.

## Your library stays with you

The books you add, your shelves, ratings, reading progress, notes and quotes, reading
sessions, finish dates and reading goal are stored on your device. The home screen widget
reads this information from your device as well.

If you're signed in to iCloud, Booky keeps a copy in your **private iCloud database**, so
your library stays the same on all your devices and comes back if you reinstall the app.
This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Only you can access it.
We, the developers of Booky, never receive it and cannot see it.

You can turn this off in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**. To delete your Booky data
from iCloud, go to **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Booky**.

## Searching for books

To find books, Booky uses the [Google Books API](https://developers.google.com/books).
When you search, the words you type (or the ISBN number you scan) are sent to Google so it
can return matching books. Book covers are also loaded from Google.

Google handles these requests under its own
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Booky does not send Google any other
information about you or your library.

## Camera

If you use the barcode scanner, Booky asks for access to your camera. The camera is used
only on your iPhone, to read the ISBN barcode on a book. No photos or video are saved or
sent anywhere. Only the ISBN number is used, to search Google Books as described above.

You can turn off camera access at any time in **Settings → Booky**.

## Children

Booky does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
